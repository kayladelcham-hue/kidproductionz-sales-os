iPhone & iPad

  1. Open KP Sales OS using the button above in Safari.
  2. Tap Share → Add to Home Screen.
  3. Keep Open as Web App on if shown, then tap Add.

Launch it from the KP logo on your Home Screen.

Mac

  1. Open KP Sales OS above in Safari.
  2. Choose File → Add to Dock on supported macOS versions.
  3. Keep the name KP Sales OS and choose Add.

Chrome and Edge also support installation from their address bar or app menu.

Windows PC

  1. Open this page in Edge or Chrome.
  2. Use Install KP Sales OS above when available, or the browser’s app-install option.
  3. Pin the installed app to your Start menu or taskbar.

In Edge, look under Apps in the browser menu. In Chrome, look under Cast, save and share.

Your data and account

This installs the web app. It uses the same live leads, account and connections. You may need to sign in when opening it on another device. An internet connection is required to load and save sales data.

It does not download a separate database. App Store and native installer packages are not included.

Already installed? Open KP Sales OS from your device’s app icon.
